## Scope

URadar is a venture platform I’m helping push toward a real business in agentic social intelligence for workspace security.

The product direction is different from traditional awareness-training tools. Instead of starting with simulated attacks and assigning training afterward, URadar is shaped around real behavior inside collaboration channels: what people share, how risky actions appear in context, and how the system can intervene without pulling users out of the place where work is already happening.

## My Role

I treat this as real ownership work, not just feature delivery. My role is closest to technical lead: helping shape architecture, technical direction, delivery standards, and the way the platform evolves in practice.

## Product Shape

At a high level, the platform brings together:

- passive monitoring of workplace communication channels
- contextual, in-channel nudges when risky behavior appears
- conversational diagnostics for incidents that need more context
- behavior-driven learning content instead of generic catalog-first training
- tenant-specific guidance grounded in company policies and internal docs
- multi-tenant architecture for organizations, MSPs, and reseller-style operations

The engineering challenge is not only detecting risky content. It is designing a system that can observe behavior, reason about context, isolate tenant data, guide users at the right moment, and produce useful operational evidence without turning every interaction into noise.

## What I’m Learning Through It

This project is one of the strongest sources of current engineering growth for me because it forces me to think beyond one stack or one feature at a time.

- multi-tenant architecture and access boundaries
- authentication and identity flows
- collaboration-channel integrations
- retrieval-augmented guidance grounded in tenant-specific knowledge
- production deployment and operational practices
- infrastructure learning that is taking me deeper into AWS concerns like networking, runtime hardening, and environment design

## Why It Matters

The value of URadar, for me, is that it sits at the intersection of product, engineering, quality, and business reality. It keeps teaching me how technical decisions hold up when the product has to support real organizations, real communication patterns, and real operational expectations.
