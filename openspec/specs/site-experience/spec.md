# Site Experience

## Purpose

Goatveloper presents a writing-first personal software engineering site. The experience should feel authored, modern, calm, and technically serious without becoming flashy or generic.

## Header and navigation

- Desktop navigation must remain compact and visible in the header.
- Mobile navigation must collapse into a hamburger button.
- The mobile menu must expose the primary navigation links in an accessible disclosure-style panel.
- The mobile menu must support clear open/close state, keyboard use, and closing on Escape and navigation selection.
- The manual theme toggle must remain available in both desktop and mobile header layouts.

## Homepage

- The homepage must immediately identify Agustin as the person behind the site.
- The hero must communicate a writing-first positioning rather than a generic portfolio pitch.
- The hero must include direct entry points into reading and profile/resume material.
- The homepage may use a subtle mesh-like atmospheric background, but only in a low-contrast, reduced-motion-safe way.
- The homepage atmospheric layer should feel page-level rather than trapped inside a single content block.
- The homepage should feel more editorial and asymmetrical than the inner pages.
- The homepage should avoid repeated dashboard-like cards and repeated three-column summary patterns.
- The homepage may feature one pinned note and one current project, but these should read like editorial selections rather than product cards.
- The homepage may use section-level scroll reveal and a subtle guided dashed path through whitespace, but only in a restrained way.
- Sections that have no content to show should not render as empty layouts.

## Shared visual system

- Light and dark themes must both feel polished and intentional.
- The site should use Instrument Sans for interface/display typography and Source Serif 4 for reading-oriented copy and prose.
- Typography, spacing, and section rhythm must emphasize readability and hierarchy.
- The palette should feel editorial and authored rather than startup-like, with a restrained copper-gold accent.
- Major section dividers may extend beyond the constrained content width when that better supports the editorial composition.
- Motion must remain restrained and useful, such as subtle hover, toggle, or panel transitions.
- Reduced-motion preferences must be respected.

## Supporting pages

- Articles index and projects index should behave like publication/archive pages rather than card-first grids.
- About and resume pages should rely on narrative sections and strong typography more than decorative side panels.
- The About page should support a portrait alongside the main introduction when a public portrait image is available.
- Sparse article and project indexes may use a pinned/spotlight treatment so the page still feels intentional with very little content.
- Article and project indexes should provide explicit empty states when no entries are available.
- Article detail pages must preserve readable prose, TOC behavior, code-copy affordances, and clear metadata.
