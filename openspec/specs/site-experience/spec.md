# Site Experience

## Purpose

Goatveloper presents a writing-first personal software engineering site. The experience should feel authored, modern, calm, and technically serious without becoming flashy or generic.

## Header and navigation

- Desktop navigation must remain compact and visible in the header.
- Mobile navigation must collapse into a hamburger button.
- The mobile menu must expose the primary navigation links in an accessible disclosure-style panel.
- The mobile menu must support clear open/close state, keyboard use, and closing on Escape and navigation selection.
- The manual theme toggle must remain available in both desktop and mobile header layouts.

## Homepage

- The homepage must immediately identify Agustin as the person behind the site.
- The hero must communicate a writing-first positioning rather than a generic portfolio pitch.
- The hero must include direct entry points into reading and profile/resume material.
- The hero must include authored supporting context such as profile facts, current focus areas, or the site's editorial framing.
- The homepage must avoid broken or cramped supporting-list layouts on mobile.
- The homepage may include supporting navigation cards, a featured article, and project previews, but these should remain easy to scan.
- Sections that have no content to show should not render as empty layouts.

## Shared visual system

- Light and dark themes must both feel polished and intentional.
- Typography, spacing, and section rhythm must emphasize readability and hierarchy.
- Motion must remain restrained and useful, such as subtle hover, toggle, or panel transitions.
- Reduced-motion preferences must be respected.

## Supporting pages

- Articles index, projects index, about, and resume pages should share the same editorial rhythm as the stronger article-detail experience.
- Sparse article and project indexes may use an overview panel and spotlight treatment so the page still feels intentional with very little content.
- Article and project indexes should provide explicit empty states when no entries are available.
- Article detail pages must preserve readable prose, TOC behavior, code-copy affordances, and clear metadata.
