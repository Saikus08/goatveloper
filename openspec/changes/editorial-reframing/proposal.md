# Editorial Reframing

## Why

The previous refresh improved structure and responsiveness, but it still feels too safe, too templated, and too close to a polished AI-generated portfolio.

The user wants the site to feel more authored, more memorable, and less like a SaaS/product-marketing design system. The current issues are:

- the palette feels too synthetic and startup-like
- repeated page structures make the site feel formulaic
- repeated card/grid patterns make the site feel generic
- too much connective copy sounds generalized instead of authored
- the current design does not yet use expressive visual atmosphere on the homepage

The revised direction is:

- authored editorial engineer
- expressive homepage, calmer reading pages
- Instrument Sans for UI/display and Source Serif 4 for reading
- dark/light palette built around the existing B direction, but with a copper-gold accent instead of the current teal-first system
- less repetition, fewer cards, fewer side-panels
- subtle mesh-like motion on the homepage only

## What Changes

This change reframes Goatveloper away from a polished portfolio-template feel and toward an authored technical publication.

Planned updates:

1. Introduce a two-font system: Instrument Sans for interface/display and Source Serif 4 for prose.
2. Replace the current palette with a darker editorial palette using a restrained copper-gold accent.
3. Add a subtle, reduced-motion-safe mesh/atmospheric moving background to the homepage hero only.
4. Redesign homepage composition to feel more editorial and asymmetrical, with fewer boxed sections.
5. Reduce repeated title-plus-panel and repeated three-column layout patterns across the site.
6. Shift article/project indexes toward more publication-style structure rather than card-first grids.
7. Tighten or remove generic connective copy so the site feels more specific and authored.

## Scope

In scope:

- homepage visual atmosphere and motion
- shared typography system
- shared color tokens and surfaces
- header and homepage composition refinements
- article/project listing structure refinements
- about/resume structure refinements where needed
- supporting CSS/component changes required to remove repetitive layouts

Out of scope for this change:

- final content backfill from external repos, old articles, resume, and degree details
- final brand/logo vectorization
- adding heavy animation or decorative effects to reading pages

## Expected Result

After this change, Goatveloper should feel like an authored engineering publication with a stronger point of view: better typography, a less synthetic palette, a more memorable homepage, calmer inner pages, and less template-like repetition.

## Risks

- homepage atmosphere could become distracting if motion is too visible
- expressive design could hurt article readability if applied too broadly
- typography changes could reduce technical clarity if the pair is not tuned carefully

## Mitigations

- keep motion low-contrast and homepage-only
- keep article pages calmer than the homepage
- preserve strong reading defaults, spacing, and code readability
- respect reduced-motion preferences and accessible contrast
